# Storylines

Three different films for the tiny-agents product demo. The picked one is written
out shot by shot in `STORYBOARD.md`. Nothing is built for the other two.

The one thing, in the viewer's words: **"My bots do real work while I'm away, and
ask me before anything that matters."**

## A. The inbox (recommended)

- **Device and form:** the result first; one home screen the film keeps returning to.
- **What the viewer sees:** the app's own bot list, like a messenger inbox, filling
  with unread replies from bots that finished real jobs. Each row is opened in turn
  and the film shows how that bot got it done, then returns to the list.
- **First two seconds:** the bot list at 6:42 PM. Four unread previews land one
  after another: "Fixed the flaky checkout test. 48 passing." / "Goa fares dropped
  ₹4,200." / an amber "waiting on you" mark / "Expense Tracker is ready."
- **Beats:** inbox hook → name → coding hand-off → scheduled browsing with take
  control → approval from the phone with the Vault → a bot that builds a bot →
  group chat → and more → inbox all read → end card.
- **Look:** the app's light theme on the site's white and graphite, Inter, green for
  done and amber for waiting; the end card flips to the brand's near-black.
- **Sound:** a light 120 BPM bed; a message pop per preview; clicks on the cursor.

Why this one: the bot list is the product's real identity ("a messenger where every
contact is an AI bot"), it shows four payoffs before explaining anything, and it
recomposes for vertical without a redesign because a chat list is already a phone
screen.

## B. One bug, start to finish

- **Device and form:** one object across the product; one continuous take.
- **What the viewer sees:** a single failing test travels through everything: asked
  from the phone, handed to Claude Code on the sandbox, an approval on the phone,
  the fix checked in the bot's live browser, a teammate pulled into the chat.
- **First two seconds:** a red failing test fills the frame and turns green.
- **Beats:** red to green → rewind → ask → code → approve → verify live → share → end card.
- **Look and sound:** as A, with the terminal's dark surface as a second world.

Deeper on coding and the tightest story, but it shows the product as a coding tool
only: no routines, no bot builder, no everyday tasks. Best at 40 to 50 s.

## C. The night shift (the bold one)

- **Device and form:** life before and after; a clock that never stops.
- **What the viewer sees:** the laptop closes at 11 PM. A clock runs through the
  night while the bots keep working in their browser, terminal and Code threads, and
  one push lights a phone on a nightstand. The laptop opens at 7 AM on finished work.
- **First two seconds:** a laptop lid closes; the screen behind it keeps moving.
- **Beats:** lid closes → 1 AM routine → 3 AM code → 5 AM approval waits → 7 AM lid opens → end card.
- **Look and sound:** night blue-black turning to morning light; a slow build that
  opens up at dawn.

The most memorable picture and the best case for "always on", but fewer features
read clearly and the laptop and nightstand would be drawn, not real product.
