Tiny Agents

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

Tiny Agents is a self-hosted AI assistant from Nesin Technologies LLP. This policy covers the Tiny Agents apps — the Android app (io.nesin.tinyagents), the web app, and any future iOS app — and the tiny-agents.nesin.io website.

Your data stays on your server

Tiny Agents is designed so your data stays with you. All of your content — conversations, memories, and the vault — is stored in an SQLite database on your own server, which you host and control. It is never sent to Nesin Technologies LLP.

What the app connects to

  • Your own server. The apps talk directly to the server you configure, with no intermediary run by us.
  • Your model providers. When you send a message, the server forwards it to the model provider(s) you configure — such as Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google Gemini — using your own API keys. Those requests go directly to the provider and are governed by the provider's own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect your personal data.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.
  • The apps contain no analytics and no advertising.
  • We have no access to your conversations, memories, or vault.

Website

This website uses a privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics service (self-hosted Plausible). It does not use cookies and does not track or identify you.

Children

Tiny Agents is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
